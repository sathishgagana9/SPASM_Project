1.  **Perform a More Thorough Furnace Reset:** Sometimes, internal safety features require a longer power cycle to fully reset.
    *   Please locate the light switch near your furnace (or the circuit breaker for the furnace) and turn it **OFF**.

2.  **Check the Condensate Drain and Safety Switch (for high-efficiency furnaces):** Many modern, high-efficiency furnaces produce condensation during operation.     *   **Safety First:** Ensure the power to the furnace is *OFF* (if you haven't already from step 1).
