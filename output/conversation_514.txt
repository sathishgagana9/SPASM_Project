### Overdraft Protection Options

Avoiding overdraft fees is a huge priority for many, and we offer several ways to help you manage that:

1.  **Overdraft Transfer from Savings:** This is a very common and effective option. You can link your checking account to your savings account (or another eligible account). 2.  **Overdraft Line of Credit:** For eligible customers, you can set up a line of credit that acts as a backup. If you overdraw your checking account, funds are automatically drawn from this line of credit. You then pay back the amount borrowed, plus any interest, similar to a small loan.
3.  **Opt-in for Debit Card & ATM Transactions (Overdraft Privilege/Courtesy Pay):** By default, many banks will decline debit card purchases or ATM withdrawals that would cause an overdraft to help you avoid fees. However, you can typically *opt-in* for us to cover these transactions for a fee. While this ensures your transaction goes through, it will trigger an overdraft fee for each instance. It's a choice many make for convenience, but it's important to understand the fees involved.

The best strategy is always to monitor your balance closely, but having these protections in place can offer a safety net.
